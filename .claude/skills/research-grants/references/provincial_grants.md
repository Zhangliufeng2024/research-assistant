# Chinese Provincial and Municipal Grant Programs (中国省市基金)

## Overview
In addition to NSFC (national), Chinese researchers can apply for provincial and municipal science and technology programs. These have higher success rates and are important for building research track records before applying to NSFC.

## Zhejiang Province (浙江省)

### Zhejiang Provincial Natural Science Foundation (浙江省自然科学基金)
- **Funding Agency**: 浙江省科学技术厅 (Zhejiang Provincial Department of Science and Technology)
- **Website**: https://zjnsf.kjt.zj.gov.cn
- **Programs**:
  - **General Program (一般项目)**: 80-100K RMB, 2-3 years, ~25% success rate
  - **Youth Fund (青年基金)**: 50-80K RMB, 2-3 years, under 35 (men) / 38 (women), ~30% success rate
  - **Key Program (重点项目)**: 200-300K RMB, 3 years, ~15% success rate
  - **Major Program (重大项目)**: 500K-1M RMB, 3-5 years, invitation only
  - **Joint Fund (联合基金)**: Co-funded with enterprises, 100-200K RMB
- **Review Criteria**: Innovation (35%), Research Foundation (25%), Feasibility (25%), Budget Reasonableness (15%)
- **Priority Areas (工程相关)**:
  - 新型建筑工业化 (New Construction Industrialization)
  - 智能建造与建筑机器人 (Intelligent Construction and Construction Robots)
  - 绿色建筑与建筑节能 (Green Building and Energy Efficiency)
  - 基础设施智能运维 (Smart Infrastructure O&M)
  - 水环境治理与生态修复 (Water Environment Treatment and Ecological Restoration)
  - 碳达峰碳中和关键技术 (Key Technologies for Carbon Peak and Neutrality)
  - 人工智能 (Artificial Intelligence)
- **Application Period**: Usually March-April each year
- **Language**: Chinese (bilingual abstract)

### Zhejiang Provincial Key R&D Program (浙江省重点研发计划)
- **Budget**: 1-5M RMB per project, 2-3 years
- **Focus**: Applied research, technology development, industrial application
- **Sub-programs**:
  - "领雁"计划: High-level talent teams
  - "尖兵"计划: Key technology breakthroughs
  - Social development: Environmental protection, urban construction
- **Requirement**: Industry collaboration encouraged, matching funds from enterprises

## Ningbo City (宁波市)

### Ningbo Natural Science Foundation (宁波市自然科学基金)
- **Funding Agency**: 宁波市科学技术局 (Ningbo Municipal Bureau of Science and Technology)
- **Programs**:
  - **General Program (一般项目)**: 50-80K RMB, 2 years, ~30% success rate
  - **Youth Fund (青年基金)**: 30-50K RMB, 2 years, under 35, ~35% success rate
- **Review Criteria**: Innovation (30%), Feasibility (30%), Research Foundation (25%), Application Value (15%)
- **Priority Areas**: New materials, intelligent manufacturing, marine technology, environmental protection
- **Application Period**: Usually January-February

### Ningbo Key S&T Program (宁波市科技计划重点项目)
- **Budget**: 200K-1M RMB, 2-3 years
- **Focus**: Local economic development, industrial upgrading
- **Requirement**: Local institution affiliation required

## Jiangsu Province (江苏省)

### Jiangsu Provincial Natural Science Foundation (江苏省自然科学基金)
- **Programs**:
  - **General Program**: 100-150K RMB, 3 years
  - **Youth Fund**: 50-100K RMB, 3 years
  - **Key Program**: 300-500K RMB, 3 years
- **Priority Areas**: Advanced manufacturing, new materials, biomedicine, environmental protection
- **Application Period**: Usually February-March

### Jiangsu Provincial Key R&D Program (江苏省重点研发计划)
- **Budget**: 1-5M RMB, 2-3 years
- **Focus**: Industrial technology innovation, social development

## Application Strategy for Provincial Grants

### When to Apply
1. **Career Stage**: Provincial Youth Fund → NSFC Youth Fund → Provincial General → NSFC General
2. **Timeline**: Provincial grants typically have shorter review cycles (3-6 months vs. 6-12 for NSFC)
3. **Success Rate**: Provincial grants generally 25-35% vs. NSFC 20-25%

### Cross-Provincial Applications
- Some provinces allow applications from researchers at institutions in neighboring provinces (check specific rules)
- Industry collaboration often strengthens provincial applications
- Local government matching funds may be available

### Writing Tips
- Emphasize local economic/social relevance (more than NSFC)
- Include expected technology transfer or industry application
- Budget is usually simpler than NSFC (fewer categories)
- Progress reports required annually (stricter than NSFC)
- Many provinces now require Chinese-language proposals only

### Key Differences from NSFC
| Aspect | NSFC | Provincial |
|--------|------|-----------|
| Language | Chinese + English abstract | Chinese only |
| Budget | Detailed categories | Simplified |
| Review | Expert peer review | Panel + correspondence |
| Duration | 3 years (typical) | 2-3 years |
| Matching | Not required | Sometimes required |
| Local relevance | Not emphasized | Strongly emphasized |
| Success rate | 20-25% | 25-35% |
