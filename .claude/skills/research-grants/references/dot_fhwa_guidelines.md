# DOT/FHWA (Department of Transportation / Federal Highway Administration) Grant Writing Guidelines

## Agency Overview

**Mission**: To ensure a fast, safe, efficient, accessible, and convenient transportation system that meets vital national interests and enhances quality of life

**Annual Budget**: ~$100 billion (all modes), ~$60 billion (FHWA)

**Website**: https://www.transportation.gov | https://www.fhwa.dot.gov

**Key Characteristics**:
- Focus on applied research with implementation potential
- Strong emphasis on safety, infrastructure, and mobility
- Partnership with state DOTs and universities
- Research priorities driven by national transportation needs
- Implementation and deployment are key outcomes
- Equity and sustainability are increasing priorities

## DOT Funding Programs

### 1. University Transportation Centers (UTC)

**Amount**: $1-4 million per year per center

**Duration**: 5 years (competitively selected)

**Focus**: Transportation research, education, and technology transfer

**Requirements**:
- Minimum 2 universities per center
- Industry partnerships required
- Education and workforce development plan
- Technology transfer and implementation plan

**Tier Structure**:
- **Tier 1**: $4M/year, national scope, 3+ universities
- **Tier 2**: $2M/year, regional scope, 2+ universities
- **Tier 3**: $1M/year, local scope, 2+ universities

### 2. State Planning and Research (SPR)

**Amount**: Formula-based to states (~2% of federal highway funds)

**Focus**: State-specific transportation research priorities

**Process**:
- State DOTs develop research programs
- Universities often serve as subcontractors
- Research must address state priorities

### 3. High Priority Research (HPR)

**Amount**: Varies by program

**Focus**: National transportation research priorities

**Topics**:
- Infrastructure resilience
- Safety innovations
- Connected and automated vehicles
- Equity in transportation
- Climate and sustainability

### 4. SBIR (Small Business Innovation Research)

**Phase I**: Up to $200K for 6 months
**Phase II**: Up to $1.5M for 2 years

**Focus**: Small business innovations in transportation

### 5. Other Programs

- **FHWA Cooperative Research**: Turner-Fairbank Highway Research Center
- **FTA Research**: Public transit innovations
- **NHTSA Research**: Traffic safety
- **FAA Research**: Aviation safety and efficiency
- **MARAD Research**: Maritime transportation

## UTC Proposal Structure

### Required Sections

1. **Executive Summary**
   - Research focus and vision
   - Key research themes
   - Expected outcomes

2. **Research Program**
   - Research themes and priorities
   - Specific research projects
   - Methodology and approach
   - Timeline and milestones

3. **Education and Workforce Development**
   - Student training programs
   - Curriculum development
   - Workforce pipeline
   - Diversity and inclusion

4. **Technology Transfer and Implementation**
   - Implementation strategy
   - Stakeholder engagement
   - Knowledge dissemination
   - Deployment support

5. **Institutional Capacity**
   - Faculty expertise
   - Facilities and equipment
   - Current research portfolio
   - Industry partnerships

6. **Management Plan**
   - Organization structure
   - Decision-making process
   - Communication plan
   - Performance metrics

7. **Budget and Cost Sharing**
   - Detailed budget
   - Cost sharing (required)
   - Leveraging resources

### Budget Considerations

| Category | Typical Percentage | Notes |
|----------|-------------------|-------|
| Personnel | 50-60% | Faculty, students, staff |
| Equipment | 10-15% | Testing equipment, sensors |
| Travel | 5-10% | Conferences, field work |
| Supplies | 10-15% | Materials, consumables |
| Other | 5-10% | Publication, training |
| Indirect | Per rate | Institutional F&A rate |
| Cost Sharing | 20-50% | Required for UTC |

## DOT Review Criteria

### For UTC Proposals

1. **Research Excellence** (30%)
   - Quality and innovation of proposed research
   - Alignment with transportation priorities
   - Methodological rigor
   - Expected outcomes and impact

2. **Education and Workforce** (25%)
   - Student training programs
   - Curriculum development
   - Diversity and inclusion
   - Workforce pipeline

3. **Technology Transfer** (20%)
   - Implementation strategy
   - Stakeholder engagement
   - Knowledge dissemination
   - Deployment support

4. **Institutional Capacity** (15%)
   - Faculty expertise
   - Facilities and equipment
   - Industry partnerships
   - Track record

5. **Management and Budget** (10%)
   - Management structure
   - Budget reasonableness
   - Cost sharing commitment
   - Performance metrics

### For SPR Proposals

1. **Relevance**: Alignment with state transportation priorities
2. **Technical Merit**: Soundness of research methodology
3. **Feasibility**: Realistic timeline and budget
4. **Implementation**: Path to practice

## DOT Writing Tips

### Emphasize Implementation

- Show how research will be deployed
- Include implementation timeline
- Describe partnerships with DOTs
- Address barriers to implementation

### Stakeholder Engagement

- Identify key stakeholders (DOTs, MPOs, industry)
- Describe engagement strategy
- Include letters of support
- Show demand for research outcomes

### Safety Focus

- Transportation safety is always a priority
- Connect research to safety outcomes
- Include quantitative safety metrics
- Address vulnerable road users

### Equity and Sustainability

- Address equity in transportation access
- Consider environmental sustainability
- Include climate resilience
- Address underserved communities

### Safety

- Driver behavior
- Pedestrian and cyclist safety
- Work zone safety
- Connected and automated vehicles

### Mobility

- Traffic operations
- Transit planning
- Multimodal transportation
- Freight and logistics
- Demand management

### Technology

- Connected and automated vehicles
- Intelligent transportation systems
- Data analytics
- Electric vehicles and charging
- Emerging technologies

## DOT vs. NSF Comparison

| Aspect | DOT/FHWA | NSF |
|--------|----------|-----|
| Focus | Applied research | Basic research |
| Implementation | Required | Not required |
| Stakeholders | DOTs, industry | Academic community |
| Timeline | Shorter (2-3 years) | Longer (3-5 years) |
| Cost Sharing | Often required | Not required |
| Safety | Primary concern | Not emphasized |
| Equity | Increasing priority | Broader impacts |
