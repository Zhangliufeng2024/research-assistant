# Funding Mechanisms Guide

## Overview

Understanding the different funding mechanisms available helps you choose the right opportunity for your research stage, career level, and project scope.

## NSF Funding Mechanisms

### Standard Research Grants

- **Amount**: Up to $500K, typically 3-5 years
- **Eligibility**: Any researcher at a US institution
- **Review**: Panel review with two criteria (Intellectual Merit + Broader Impacts)
- **Best for**: Established researchers with clear research programs

### CAREER Awards

- **Amount**: $400-500K over 5 years
- **Eligibility**: Early-career faculty (within 10 years of PhD)
- **Requirements**: Integrated research and education plan
- **Best for**: Assistant professors building their research program
- **Key**: Education plan must be substantive, not an afterthought

### RAPID (Grants for Rapid Response Research)

- **Amount**: Up to $200K for 1 year
- **Eligibility**: For urgent research (natural disasters, pandemics, etc.)
- **Review**: Internal review, no panel
- **Best for**: Time-sensitive research that can't wait for regular review

### EAGER (EArly-concept Grants for Exploratory Research)

- **Amount**: Up to $300K for 1-2 years
- **Eligibility**: For high-risk, high-reward exploratory research
- **Review**: Internal review
- **Best for**: Novel, potentially transformative ideas in early stages

### Collaborative Research

- **Amount**: Varies by program
- **Eligibility**: Multi-institutional teams
- **Structure**: Lead institution submits, subcontracts to collaborators
- **Best for**: Large, interdisciplinary projects

### I-Corps

- **Amount**: $50K (Teams), $500K (Sites)
- **Purpose**: Translate research into commercial applications
- **Requirements**: Customer discovery interviews (100+ for Teams)
- **Best for**: Researchers with marketable technology

## NIH Funding Mechanisms

### R01 (Research Project Grant)

- **Amount**: No fixed limit, typically $250K-$500K/year direct costs
- **Duration**: 3-5 years
- **Eligibility**: Any researcher
- **Review**: Study section with 5 scored criteria
- **Best for**: Established research programs

### R21 (Exploratory/Developmental Grant)

- **Amount**: Up to $275K total over 2 years ($200K direct + $75K indirect)
- **Duration**: 2 years
- **Eligibility**: Any researcher
- **Best for**: Novel, exploratory research; preliminary data collection
- **Note**: Cannot be renewed; for new ideas only

### R03 (Small Grant)

- **Amount**: Up to $100K total over 2 years
- **Duration**: 2 years
- **Eligibility**: Any researcher
- **Best for**: Small projects, pilot studies, method development

### R15 (AREA - Academic Research Enhancement Award)

- **Amount**: Up to $300K total over 3 years
- **Eligibility**: Primarily undergraduate institutions
- **Best for**: Faculty at teaching-focused institutions

### R35 (MIRA - Maximizing Investigators' Research Award)

- **Amount**: Up to $750K direct costs per year
- **Duration**: 5 years
- **Eligibility**: Established investigators
- **Best for**: Funding the investigator, not a specific project

### K Awards (Career Development)

- **K01**: Mentored Research Scientist Development Award
- **K08**: Mentored Clinical Scientist Research Career Development Award
- **K23**: Mentored Patient-Oriented Research Career Development Award
- **K99/R00**: Pathway to Independence Award
- **Best for**: Postdocs and early-career researchers transitioning to independence

### F Awards (Fellowships)

- **F30**: Predoctoral MD/PhD Fellowship
- **F31**: Predoctoral Fellowship (PhD students)
- **F32**: Postdoctoral Fellowship
- **Best for**: Graduate students and postdocs

### P01 (Program Project Grant)

- **Amount**: Varies, typically large
- **Duration**: 5 years
- **Structure**: Multiple projects with a central theme
- **Best for**: Large, multi-PI research programs

## DOE Funding Mechanisms

### Office of Science (SC)

**Programs**:
- **ASCR**: Advanced Scientific Computing Research
- **BES**: Basic Energy Sciences
- **BER**: Biological and Environmental Research
- **FES**: Fusion Energy Sciences
- **HEP**: High Energy Physics
- **NP**: Nuclear Physics

**Amount**: Varies by program, typically $100K-$500K/year
**Duration**: 3 years (typical)

### ARPA-E (Advanced Research Projects Agency - Energy)

- **Amount**: Varies by program
- **Duration**: 2-4 years
- **Focus**: Transformative energy technologies
- **Requirements**: Cost sharing (typically 20-50%)
- **Best for**: High-risk, high-reward energy research

### EERE (Energy Efficiency and Renewable Energy)

**Programs**:
- Solar Energy Technologies
- Wind Energy Technologies
- Water Power Technologies
- Geothermal Technologies
- Building Technologies
- Advanced Manufacturing
- Vehicle Technologies
- Bioenergy Technologies
- Hydrogen and Fuel Cell Technologies

**Amount**: Varies by program
**Focus**: Applied research and development

### Early Career Research Program

- **Amount**: $750K over 5 years
- **Eligibility**: Within 10 years of PhD
- **Best for**: Early-career researchers in DOE mission areas

## DARPA Funding Mechanisms

### Broad Agency Announcements (BAAs)

**Types**:
- **Program BAAs**: Specific program with defined goals
- **Office-Wide BAAs**: Broader topics within an office

**Amount**: Varies by program, typically $1M-$10M+
**Duration**: 3-5 years, phased

### SBIR (Small Business Innovation Research)

- **Phase I**: $150-250K for 6-12 months
- **Phase II**: $750K-$1.5M for 2 years
- **Phase III**: Commercialization (no SBIR funding)
- **Eligibility**: Small businesses (<500 employees)

### Young Faculty Award (YFA)

- **Amount**: Up to $500K over 2 years
- **Eligibility**: Tenure-track faculty within 5 years of PhD
- **Best for**: Early-career researchers in DARPA mission areas

### Director's Fellowship

- **Amount**: Varies
- **Eligibility**: Exceptional performers from prior DARPA programs
- **Best for**: Proven performers seeking continued funding

## Engineering-Specific Funding

### DOT/FHWA (Department of Transportation)

**State Planning and Research (SPR)**:
- **Amount**: Formula-based to states
- **Focus**: State-specific transportation research priorities
- **Eligibility**: State DOTs (universities as subcontractors)

**University Transportation Centers (UTC)**:
- **Amount**: $1-4M/year per center
- **Duration**: 5 years
- **Focus**: Transportation research, education, and technology transfer
- **Eligibility**: Competitively selected universities

**High Priority Research (HPR)**:
- **Amount**: Varies
- **Focus**: National transportation research priorities

### USACE (US Army Corps of Engineers)

- **Amount**: Varies by program
- **Focus**: Civil works, water resources, coastal engineering
- **Programs**: ERDC (Engineer Research and Development Center)

### EPA (Environmental Protection Agency)

- **Amount**: Varies by program
- **Focus**: Environmental research, air quality, water quality
- **Programs**: STAR (Science to Achieve Results) grants

### State DOTs

- **Amount**: Varies by state
- **Focus**: State-specific transportation needs
- **Process**: Typically through state DOT research divisions

## CS-Specific Funding

### NSF CISE Directorate

**Divisions**:
- **CCF**: Computing and Communication Foundations
- **CNS**: Computer and Network Systems
- **IIS**: Information and Intelligent Systems
- **OAC**: Office of Advanced Cyberinfrastructure

**Programs**:
- Core programs in each division
- CAREER awards
- Medium/Large collaborative grants
- SaTC (Secure and Trustworthy Cyberspace)
- BIGDATA
- HDR (Harnessing the Data Revolution)

### DARPA I2O (Information Innovation Office)

- **Focus**: AI, cybersecurity, data analytics
- **Programs**: Various BAAs
- **Best for**: High-risk, high-reward computing research

### DOE ASCR (Advanced Scientific Computing Research)

- **Focus**: Computing, data, networking
- **Programs**: SciDAC, ASCR Leadership Computing
- **Best for**: High-performance computing research

## International Funding

### NSFC (National Natural Science Foundation of China)

**Programs**:
- **General Program** (面上项目): 3 years, ~600-800K RMB
- **Young Scientists Fund** (青年基金): 3 years, ~200-300K RMB
- **Key Program** (重点项目): 4-5 years, ~3-5M RMB
- **Major Program** (重大项目): 5 years, ~10-20M RMB
- **International Cooperation** (国际合作): Joint funding with foreign agencies

**Eligibility**:
- Young Scientists Fund: Men <35, Women <40
- General Program: No age limit
- Must be affiliated with a Chinese institution

### EU Horizon Europe

**Pillars**:
1. **Excellent Science**: ERC grants, Marie Skłodowska-Curie actions
2. **Global Challenges**: Clusters (Health, Digital, Energy, etc.)
3. **Innovative Europe**: EIC Accelerator

**Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA)**:
- **Postdoctoral Fellowships**: 1-2 years, ~€150K/year
- **Doctoral Networks**: Training networks for PhD students
- **Staff Exchanges**: Short-term exchanges between institutions

### UKRI (UK Research and Innovation)

- **EPSRC**: Engineering and Physical Sciences Research Council
- **NERC**: Natural Environment Research Council
- **AHRC**: Arts and Humanities Research Council
- **Amount**: Varies by program
- **Duration**: Typically 3-5 years

## Choosing the Right Mechanism

### Decision Framework

1. **Career Stage**: Student → Postdoc → Early Career → Mid-Career → Senior
2. **Project Stage**: Exploratory → Pilot → Full Research → Applied → Commercialization
3. **Amount Needed**: Small ($50-100K) → Medium ($100-500K) → Large ($500K+)
4. **Topic Fit**: Basic research → Applied research → Development → Deployment
5. **Timeline**: Short (1-2 years) → Medium (3-5 years) → Long (5+ years)

### Matching Table

| Career Stage | Recommended Mechanisms |
|-------------|----------------------|
| PhD Student | NSF GRFP, NIH F31, DOE NNSA SSGF |
| Postdoc | NIH F32, NSF MPS-DRF, DOE NNSA LRGF |
| Asst. Professor | NSF CAREER, NIH K99/R00, DOE ECRP, DARPA YFA |
| Assoc. Professor | NSF Standard, NIH R01, DOE BES, DARPA BAA |
| Full Professor | NIH R01, NIH R35, NSF Large, DARPA Program |
