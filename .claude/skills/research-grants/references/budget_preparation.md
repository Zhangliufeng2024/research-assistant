# Budget Preparation and Justification Guide

## Overview

A well-prepared budget demonstrates that you have thought carefully about the resources needed to accomplish your research goals. The budget must be reasonable, well-justified, and aligned with the proposed work.

## Budget Categories

### 1. Senior Personnel

**Who**: PI, Co-PIs, Senior Researchers

**Key Rules by Agency**:

| Agency | Salary Rules | Summer Salary |
|--------|-------------|---------------|
| NSF | No salary cap; 2-month limit on summer salary | Up to 2 months |
| NIH | Salary cap (~$221,900 as of 2024); proportional to effort | N/A (calendar year) |
| DOE | Follow institutional policy; cost sharing may apply | Varies by program |
| DARPA | No formal cap; must be reasonable | Per phase budget |

**Justification Strategy**:
- State effort in person-months (calendar, academic, or summer)
- Explain why each person's expertise is needed
- Show that the time commitment is realistic given other obligations

### 2. Postdoctoral Researchers

**Budget Items**:
- Salary (follow institutional norms)
- Fringe benefits (typically 25-35% of salary)
- Travel to 1-2 conferences per year
- Training and professional development

**Justification**:
- Describe the postdoc's specific role in the project
- Explain the training plan and career development opportunities
- Justify the duration of support requested

### 3. Graduate Students

**Budget Items**:
- Stipend (institutional rate, typically $25,000-$40,000/year)
- Tuition and fees (often required for NSF; NIH uses tuition remission)
- Health insurance
- Travel to conferences

**Justification**:
- Describe the student's contribution to the project
- Explain how the project aligns with their thesis research
- Note any co-advising or mentoring plans

### 4. Undergraduate Students

- Hourly wages or stipends
- Summer research experiences (REU supplements)
- Training and supervision time

### 5. Equipment

**Definition**: Items costing $5,000 or more with a useful life >1 year

**Justification Requirements**:
- Explain why the equipment is necessary for the proposed research
- Show that it is not available at the institution
- Describe who will use it and how often
- Include vendor quotes for items >$25,000

**Engineering-Specific Equipment**:
- Testing machines (MTS, Instron, shake tables)
- Sensors and data acquisition systems
- Environmental chambers
- Fabrication equipment (3D printers, CNC machines)

**CS-Specific Equipment**:
- GPU servers or computing clusters
- Specialized hardware (FPGAs, TPUs)
- Storage systems for large datasets

### 6. Travel

**Domestic Travel**:
- Conference presentations (1-2 per year)
- Collaboration visits
- Field work travel

**International Travel**:
- Must be specifically justified
- Follow Fly America Act requirements
- Include per diem rates by location

### 7. Participant Support

- Stipends for workshop participants
- Travel and subsistence for trainees
- Registration fees
- **Note**: Participant support costs are typically excluded from F&A calculation

### 8. Other Direct Costs

- Materials and supplies
- Publication costs
- Computer services and cloud computing
- Consultant services
- Subcontract costs

### 9. Indirect Costs (F&A)

- Negotiated rate with your institution
- Applied to Modified Total Direct Costs (MTDC)
- Excludes equipment, participant support, tuition, amounts >$25K per subcontract

## Agency-Specific Budget Rules

### NSF Budget

- **Duration**: Up to 5 years
- **PI Salary**: 2 months summer salary maximum
- **No modular budget**: Detailed budget required for all proposals
- **Cost sharing**: Not required (and generally not allowed)
- **Budget justification**: Up to 3 pages

### NIH Budget

- **Modular budget**: For applications ≤$250K direct costs per year
- **Detailed budget**: For applications >$250K
- **Modular format**: $25K increments, no line items needed
- **Consortium/subcontract**: Count toward $250K threshold
- **Salary cap**: Executive Level II (~$221,900)
- **Budget justification**: No page limit (but be concise)

### DOE Budget

- **Cost sharing**: Often required (varies by program)
- **Quarterly budget breakdown**: May be required
- **National lab costs**: Include as subcontracts
- **IP provisions**: May affect budget structure

### DARPA Budget

- **Phase-based**: Budget by phase (Phase 1, 2, 3)
- **Task-based**: Budget by technical tasks
- **No formal salary cap**: Must be reasonable
- **Cost sharing**: Not typically required
- **Transition costs**: Include deployment/transition budget

## Budget Justification Writing

### Structure

1. **Personnel**: For each person, state name, role, effort, and why they are needed
2. **Equipment**: Justify each item >$5K
3. **Travel**: Specify destinations, purposes, and costs
4. **Other Direct Costs**: Itemize materials, supplies, services
5. **Subcontracts**: Justify each subcontractor's role and costs

### Writing Tips

- **Be specific**: "Dr. Smith will dedicate 1.5 academic months to Aim 1, leading the finite element analysis of bridge specimens"
- **Align with research plan**: Every budget item should connect to a specific aim or task
- **Show value**: Demonstrate that the budget is cost-effective
- **Avoid padding**: Reviewers can identify inflated budgets
- **Include quotes**: For major equipment or services

### Common Budget Mistakes

1. **Unjustified personnel**: Not explaining why each person is needed
2. **Missing fringe**: Forgetting to include fringe benefits
3. **No travel justification**: Not explaining conference choices
4. **Overbudgeting supplies**: Inflated supply costs without justification
5. **Ignoring agency rules**: NSF 2-month rule, NIH salary cap
6. **Missing indirect costs**: Forgetting F&A
7. **No escalation**: Not accounting for annual salary increases in multi-year proposals

## Engineering-Specific Budget Considerations

- **Lab access fees**: If using shared facilities
- **Specimen fabrication**: Materials and labor for test specimens
- **Field work**: Travel, equipment transport, permits
- **Software licenses**: ABAQUS, ANSYS, MATLAB, ArcGIS
- **Testing standards**: ASTM, EN standard documents
- **Safety equipment**: PPE for lab and field work

## CS-Specific Budget Considerations

- **Cloud computing**: AWS, GCP, Azure credits
- **GPU time**: Training large models
- **Data storage**: For large datasets
- **Software licenses**: Commercial tools if needed
- **Participant compensation**: For user studies
- **Open-source maintenance**: Post-grant software sustainability
