# NSFC (National Natural Science Foundation of China) Grant Writing Guidelines

## Agency Overview

**Mission**: To promote basic research, foster talent, and advance science and technology in China

**Annual Budget**: ~30 billion RMB (~$4.2 billion USD)

**Website**: https://www.nsfc.gov.cn (Chinese) | https://www.nsfc.gov.cn/english (English)

**Key Characteristics**:
- China's primary funding agency for basic research
- Emphasis on original innovation and scientific excellence
- Strong support for young scientists
- Increasing international cooperation
- Chinese-language proposals required for most programs
- Three-year grant cycles typical

## NSFC Funding Programs

### 1. General Program (面上项目)

**Amount**: 600,000 - 800,000 RMB (~$85-115K USD)

**Duration**: 3 years

**Eligibility**: Researchers at Chinese institutions

**Focus**: Free exploration of scientific questions

**Review Process**: Peer review by 3-5 experts

**Success Rate**: ~20%

### 2. Young Scientists Fund (青年基金)

**Amount**: 200,000 - 300,000 RMB (~$30-45K USD)

**Duration**: 3 years

**Eligibility**:
- Men: under 35 years old
- Women: under 40 years old

**Focus**: Support young researchers in independent research

**Success Rate**: ~25%

### 3. Key Program (重点项目)

**Amount**: 3,000,000 - 5,000,000 RMB (~$430-715K USD)

**Duration**: 4-5 years

**Eligibility**: Teams led by senior researchers

**Focus**: Major scientific questions requiring coordinated effort

**Review Process**: Panel review with site visits

### 4. Major Program (重大项目)

**Amount**: 10,000,000 - 20,000,000 RMB (~$1.4-2.8M USD)

**Duration**: 5 years

**Eligibility**: Large teams, often multi-institutional

**Focus**: National strategic needs, major scientific challenges

**Review Process**: Multi-stage review with site visits

### 5. International Cooperation (国际合作)

**Types**:
- **Joint Research Projects**: Bilateral collaborations
- **Joint Seminars**: Academic exchanges
- **Outgoing/Incoming Visits**: Researcher mobility

**Funding**: Varies by program

**Partners**: NSF (USA), DFG (Germany), JSPS (Japan), RS (UK), etc.

## NSFC Proposal Structure

### Required Sections

1. **基本信息** (Basic Information)
   - Project title (Chinese and English)
   - PI information
   - Research field classification
   - Keywords

2. **摘要与关键词** (Abstract and Keywords)
   - Chinese abstract: ~400 characters
   - English abstract: ~2000 characters
   - Keywords: 5-8

3. **立项依据** (Rationale and Significance)
   - Research background
   - Literature review
   - Scientific significance
   - Innovation points

4. **研究内容、目标及关键科学问题** (Research Content, Objectives, and Key Scientific Questions)
   - Research content
   - Overall objective
   - Key scientific questions to be solved

5. **研究方案及可行性分析** (Research Plan and Feasibility Analysis)
   - Research methodology
   - Technical approach
   - Feasibility analysis
   - Risk assessment

6. **年度研究计划及预期研究成果** (Annual Plan and Expected Outcomes)
   - Year-by-year plan
   - Expected deliverables
   - Publications and patents

7. **研究基础与工作条件** (Research Foundation and Working Conditions)
   - PI's relevant publications
   - Preliminary results
   - Laboratory facilities
   - Team composition

8. **经费申请** (Budget Request)
   - Equipment
   - Materials
   - Testing/processing
   - Travel
   - Other direct costs
   - Indirect costs

| Category | Typical Percentage | Notes |
|----------|-------------------|-------|
| 设备费 (Equipment) | 10-20% | Items >100K RMB need approval |

## NSFC Review Criteria

### Four Evaluation Dimensions

1. **创新性** (Innovation) - 30%
   - Novelty of the research question
   - Originality of the approach
   - Potential for breakthrough discoveries

2. **研究基础** (Research Foundation) - 25%
   - PI's publication record
   - Preliminary results
   - Team expertise

3. **可行性** (Feasibility) - 25%
   - Soundness of research plan
   - Reasonableness of timeline
   - Risk assessment and mitigation

4. **研究条件** (Research Conditions) - 20%
   - Laboratory facilities
   - Institutional support
   - Team composition

## NSFC Writing Tips

### Language Requirements

- **Chinese required**: Most sections must be in Chinese
- **English abstract**: Required for international visibility
- **Bilingual**: Some programs allow bilingual proposals

### Innovation Emphasis

- Clearly state what is new and original
- Distinguish from existing research
- Highlight potential breakthroughs
- Be specific about innovation points

### Young Scientists Fund

- Demonstrate independent research capability
- Show potential for future development
- Include strong preliminary results
- Emphasize career development plan

### Common Mistakes

1. **Insufficient innovation**: Too incremental
2. **Weak literature review**: Not comprehensive enough
3. **Poor feasibility**: Unrealistic timeline or methods
4. **Missing preliminary results**: No proof of concept
5. **Vague objectives**: Not specific enough
6. **Inadequate budget justification**: Not explaining costs

| Management Sciences | 管理科学部 | Project management, systems engineering |

### Priority Research Directions (工程领域)

**Civil & Structural Engineering (土木工程)**:
- 城市韧性与防灾减灾 (Urban resilience and disaster mitigation)
- 智能建造与建筑工业化 (Smart construction and industrialization)
- 既有结构性能提升与寿命延长 (Existing structure performance and longevity)
- 极端荷载下结构安全 (Structural safety under extreme loads)
- 岩土工程灾害机理与防控 (Geotechnical disaster mechanisms)
- 交通基础设施智能运维 (Intelligent infrastructure O&M)

**Environmental Engineering (环境工程)**:
- 水污染控制与资源化 (Water pollution control and resource recovery)
- 大气污染成因与控制 (Air pollution formation and control)
- 土壤污染防治与修复 (Soil contamination prevention and remediation)
- 固废减量与资源化利用 (Waste reduction and resource utilization)
- 碳中和与气候变化 (Carbon neutrality and climate change)
- 新污染物环境行为与效应 (Emerging contaminants behavior and effects)

**Computer Science (计算机科学)**:
- 人工智能基础理论 (AI foundational theory)
- 大数据与知识工程 (Big data and knowledge engineering)
- 网络空间安全 (Cyberspace security)
- 计算机体系结构 (Computer architecture)
- 软件理论与方法 (Software theory and methods)
- 人机交互与混合智能 (HCI and hybrid intelligence)

### Engineering-Specific Budget Considerations

| Item | Typical Range (RMB) | Notes |
|------|-------------------|-------|
| 大型设备 (Major equipment) | 200K-500K | Needs expert review approval |
| 材料费 (Materials) | 100K-300K | Concrete, steel, sensors, reagents |
| 测试化验加工费 (Testing) | 80K-200K | Mechanical testing, SEM, XRD, etc. |
| 劳务费 (Labor) | 50K-150K | Graduate students, lab assistants |
| 差旅费 (Travel) | 30K-80K | Field work, conferences |

**Engineering-specific tips:**
- 大型设备 (>100K RMB) requires detailed justification and equipment comparison
- 材料费 should list specific material types and quantities
- 测试化验 should specify which characterization methods (SEM, XRD, XPS, FTIR, etc.)
- Field testing and site investigation costs should be itemized separately

### Engineering Proposal Writing Tips (中文写作要点)

**立项依据 (Rationale)**:
- 引用近5年高水平文献，突出国际前沿
- 明确指出当前研究的不足和瓶颈
- 结合国家重大需求（如"十四五"规划、双碳目标）
- 创新点要具体、可验证，避免空泛描述

**研究方案 (Research Plan)**:
- 技术路线图要清晰（建议用流程图）
- 实验方案要包含具体参数和标准（ASTM、GB/T）
- 数值模拟要说明软件、本构模型、边界条件
- 包含验证方案（实验-数值对比、现场实测）

**研究基础 (Research Foundation)**:
- 列出近5年相关SCI/EI论文（影响因子和引用次数）
- 展示已有实验设备和测试条件
- 提供预实验结果或初步数据
- 团队成员的专业互补性

**预期成果 (Expected Outcomes)**:
- 论文数量要合理（面上：4-6篇SCI，青年：3-5篇）
- 专利申请要具体（发明/实用新型）
- 培养研究生人数
- 成果转化或工程应用前景

### NSFC Engineering Review Focus

Reviewers in engineering panels emphasize:
1. **科学问题属性**: 明确属于"鼓励探索"、"前沿交叉"、"需求牵引"或"重大突破"中的哪一类
2. **工程需求**: 是否针对实际工程问题
3. **方法创新**: 方法学上的创新优于应用创新
4. **可行性**: 实验条件和团队能力是否支撑
5. **预期影响**: 对学科发展或工程实践的推动作用

### NSFC Cross-Disciplinary Strategy (交叉学科申请策略)
- **鼓励探索类**: Novel fundamental questions at domain boundaries (e.g., E0801 × F0606 for physics-informed ML)
- **前沿交叉类**: Hot interdisciplinary topics (e.g., digital twin for smart construction = E0808 × F0606)
- **需求牵引类**: National needs driving research (e.g., carbon neutrality = E0809 × E0604)
- **重大突破类**: Major breakthroughs requiring cross-disciplinary approach
- When applying cross-disciplinary NSFC: Primary code determines the review panel. Choose the panel most likely to appreciate your methodology.
- For AI+Civil: Apply to E08 (工程科学部) with F06 as secondary — engineering reviewers will evaluate the application significance
- For AI+Environmental: Apply to E0809 with F0606 as secondary
- Budget: General Program 600-800K RMB, Young Scientists 200-300K RMB

## NSFC vs. NSF Comparison

| Aspect | NSFC | NSF |
|--------|------|-----|
| Language | Chinese | English |
| Duration | Typically 3 years | 3-5 years |
| Budget | 600K-800K RMB | Up to $500K |
| Review | Expert peer review | Panel review |
| Broader Impacts | Not emphasized | Equal weight |
| Young Scientists | Dedicated fund | CAREER award |
| International | Cooperation programs | OISE programs |
