## NSF Biosketch Writing Tips

### Products Section
- Choose publications that demonstrate expertise relevant to the proposed research
- Include a mix of journal articles, conference papers, and other relevant outputs
- Prioritize recent publications (last 5 years)
- Include DOIs for all publications
- Quality over quantity - 5 strong publications beat 10 weak ones

### Synergistic Activities Section
- Demonstrate broader impact of your work
- Show integration of research and education
- Include activities that benefit society
- Show leadership and service to the profession
- Include technology transfer and knowledge dissemination

### Common Mistakes
- Listing too many publications (stay under 10)
- Including publications not relevant to the proposed research
- Vague synergistic activities ("Taught courses")
- Missing DOIs
- Exceeding 3-page limit
