# NIH Biosketch Template (5 Pages Maximum)

## NIH Biosketch Format

### Section A: Personal Statement (4,000 character limit)

*Describe why you are well-suited for your role(s) in this project. Include relevant training, experience, and ongoing activities. Describe how your expertise will contribute to the project's goals.*

**Example**:

My research focuses on [area], with particular expertise in [specific methods/topics]. During my [X] years at [Institution], I have developed [specific capabilities] that directly align with the proposed project. My training in [relevant training] provides the foundation for [specific contribution to the project].

I have published [N] peer-reviewed articles in [relevant journals], including [key publication] which established [important finding]. My current NIH-funded project [R01 XXXX] investigates [related topic], and the proposed work extends this research by [specific extension].

My expertise in [method 1] and [method 2] will be critical for Aims 1 and 2. I have successfully mentored [N] graduate students and [N] postdoctoral researchers, and I am committed to training the next generation of [field] researchers.

## NIH Biosketch Writing Tips

### Personal Statement
- Connect your expertise directly to the specific aims
- Mention relevant training and experience
- Show how your background prepares you for this specific project
- Include mentoring philosophy if applicable
- Be specific, not generic

### Contributions to Science
- Choose contributions that demonstrate expertise relevant to the proposed work
- For each contribution, clearly state the problem, your role, and the impact
- Include PMCID numbers (required for NIH-funded publications)
- Show a progression of your research program
- Quality over quantity - 3 strong contributions beat 5 weak ones

### Common Mistakes
- Generic personal statement that could apply to any project
- Not connecting contributions to the proposed research
- Missing PMCID numbers
- Exceeding 5-page limit
- Not clearly stating your role in multi-author publications
