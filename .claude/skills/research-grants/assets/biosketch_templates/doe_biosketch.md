## DOE CV Writing Tips

- **DOE Mission Alignment**: Emphasize research relevant to DOE mission areas (energy, environment, national security)
- **Technology Readiness**: If applicable, describe experience with technology development and transfer
- **National Lab Partnerships**: Highlight any collaborations with DOE national laboratories
- **TRL Experience**: If you have experience advancing technology readiness levels, describe it
- **Cost Sharing**: If you have experience with cost-shared projects, mention it
- **Current Support**: Be thorough and accurate in listing current and pending support
