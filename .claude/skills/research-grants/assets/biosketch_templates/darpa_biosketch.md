# DARPA Team Qualifications Template

## DARPA Team Qualifications Format

*DARPA does not require a formal biosketch format. Instead, emphasize the team's ability to execute DARPA-hard research and transition technology.*

---

### Team Overview

*In 1-2 paragraphs, describe why this team is uniquely qualified to execute the proposed DARPA program. Emphasize:*
- Experience with DARPA or similar high-risk programs
- Prototype development and demonstration experience
- Technology transition track record
- Relevant technical breakthroughs

**Example**:

Our team brings together world-leading expertise in [area 1], [area 2], and [area 3]. The PI, Dr. [Name], has led [N] DARPA-funded programs, including [program name] which successfully demonstrated [achievement] and transitioned to [DoD component/commercial partner]. Our team includes experts from [institutions] with complementary capabilities in [specific areas].

### DARPA Team Qualifications Writing Tips

1. **Emphasize DARPA Experience**: If you have prior DARPA funding, highlight it prominently
2. **Show Prototype Experience**: DARPA values teams that can build and demonstrate prototypes
3. **Highlight Transitions**: Show that you can move technology from lab to operational use
4. **Be Specific About Capabilities**: Don't just list expertise, describe what you can actually do
5. **Include Industry Partners**: If you have industry partners for transition, include them
6. **Show Risk Tolerance**: Demonstrate that your team can handle high-risk, high-reward research
7. **Quantify Achievements**: Use specific numbers and metrics where possible
